# pizza product case study ground

SuperGood This is a food delivery product. Which is a sub-brand from a PizzaSushiWok company. The main competitor was Кухня на районе. The main premise is to allow people to get a homemade-like food quickly and affordable. The problem is that when I came to the company, the product was at the low. It had poor reviews about the app and about the food quality compared to the cost. I was hired as a mobile engineer, but actually I've been responsible for complete redesign across the whole application, how user itneracts and feels things like greeting screen, first experience intro screen, backet, history of orders, add and remove from order, favourites, payment order, tracking order


- identify what i was responsible for specifically, what decisions did i made that reshaped the product
- questions and answers that i got from customers 
- competitive research that ive done compared to самокат
- contextual inquiry
- and then in the exploration as ive prototyped with my cool modern redesigns that are way more plesant and more convenient than outdatess stuff, ive done task analysis a/b testing with people, we even had polls for users to pick which ones they like
- at the end the product showed easier navigation, more intuitive flows, and better usability
