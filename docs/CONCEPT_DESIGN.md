# Design concept

## Current priority and approved redesign direction

Approved by Igor on 2026-08-11.

The website's primary goal is to help Igor get hired as a product designer. Artistic expression, personality, play, and technical craft remain essential, but they must strengthen rather than delay professional understanding and access to product-design evidence.

## Concept

This is My (Igor's) personal website that is in itself also a product design case.

It shows my soul, personality, values, work as well product design abilities through the design and feel of a website itself.

This website serves two things:
1. conveying to people my soul of an artist and engineer which is for possible like-minded people.
2. as a place that stores my work for me to keep track of like what I do and what do I represent.

This website should help with:

- professional networking;
- job opportunities;
- friendship;
- possible collaborations;

The website should feel like exploring tactile interactive journey experience of mapped out print of a soul in a hand-drawn game without using game-interface chrome


It is not a conventional portfolio, résumé website, corporate landing page, or personal-brand funnel. It is an expression of my soul, the project itself.


## Intended feeling

The site should feel:

- alive;
- thoughtful;
- playful;
- technically credible;
- professional in an artistic rather than corporate way. (hand-drawn, sketchbook aesthetic elements)
- exploring the world navigiting through it by unfolding the path as you go.


## Art direction and visual language and interaction design


- Mix/blend of quite calm minimalism with maximalism "anti-portfolio" portfolio
- Restrained-with-one-loud-moment — website is primary calm and aesthetic with intentional clash with loud memorable experience (opening the box with the splash of colors)
- The balance between playful-chaotic-warm with quiet confidence that is still artistic and unique.

## Homepage product-design evidence

Approved by Igor on 2026-08-12.

- Do not alter the authored box opening or the identity composition it reveals.
- After the opening, connect the three authored values to selected professional product-design evidence rather than presenting the values as a checkbox task.
- Keep the value-word choices interactive: `experience / behaviour / capability`, `impactful / empowering`, and `clear / honest`.
- Treat concrete product decisions and behaviors as the evidence units. A project may contribute a different specific aspect to every value.
- On wide screens, keep the active value fixed in the horizontal center while its project aspects progressively appear on the left and right. Use a shorter alternating composition rather than a single long evidence column.
- On narrow screens, preserve the same centered sticky value and alternating left/right evidence entrance without horizontal document scrolling.
- Make Observatory visibly identifiable as the flagship product-design case study.
- Use Observatory, the food-ordering product, and Chinese Bee as the short homepage evidence set, followed by a clear route to the complete Work document.
- Express the short personal introduction as a concise list that can include professional disciplines and verified personal identity.
- Present work images inside substantial rounded ink frames.
- Keep typography in the values-and-evidence composition smaller than the opening typography so evidence reads as professional detail rather than a second hero.
