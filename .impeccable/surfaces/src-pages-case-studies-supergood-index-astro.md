# Case studies (pilot: SuperGood)

Scope: the four case-study routes under `/case-studies/`, piloted on SuperGood. Visitor mode: Read, with the work leading.
Audience and job: design leads hiring juniors judge outcome, role, and craft in 30 seconds, then read decisions if hooked.
Keep: information, STAR summary, decisions, metrics, Igor's writing style. Everything else is open.
Each study gets its own underneath moment chosen for its subject (Observatory: archive footage; SuperGood: focus pull, guesswork to certainty). No reused picture as decoration: SuperGood's plates are blooms of their own screens' colours.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A case study told in focus. Each decision is shown, not described: its screen large on a bloom of its own colours, arriving out of focus while the parts that changed rack sharp and are named beside it, each decision in the form that suits it (full screen, pinned, close crop, pair). Refuses the Swiss twelve-column page and the one-template plate repeated five times.

OWN-WORLD: Light field, ink text. The cover uses Igor's SuperGood card texture; below it, every plate has its own generated pigment bloom sampled from the food or map on its screen, feathered into the page with film grain only inside the colour, and the results end in one bloom of all of them. Crimson Text 400 headings, Instrument Sans everything else, study highlighter on results. Annotation = the changed regions in focus over a softly defocused screen, a dot-and-hairline leader to a short sans name; the same line links each complaint to its decision.

STORY: Outcome first, then problem, research, five decisions as plates (problem, plate, what and why, the rejected option, the result), results, reflection.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed warm out-of-focus field (SuperGood texture) with the name, pitch, outcome sentence, and role on it. Scrolling pulls the camera back and into focus until the field becomes the hero plate holding three phones.

FORM: Annotated plates, position 3 of 7 on the ranked list; seed key 29578857.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
