Imagine catching the coziest rainy night of your life. Sleeping conditions are perfect, and you have a whole night of sleep ahead of you. But then… oh no. The snoring.

Or you're in the middle of a great study session, a calm breeze outside the window, and suddenly… the construction starts.

There are a lot of moments like this, when one sound ruins everything around it. And you don't want to lose the whole moment. Just that one sound.

Right now, if your partner is snoring, you have two options. Wake them up. Not very nice. Or put on noise-cancelling headphones and play a recording of rain, which is never as alive as the real rain outside your window.

<SnoringFrequencyChart />

What if your headphones could tell sounds apart and remove only the one you choose?

The headphones listen to what's around you and recognize each sound. Open the app and you see what they're hearing right now: rain, snoring, a car passing by. Tap the snoring. It's gone. The rain stays.

You stay in control. You can see what's blocked at any moment, bring a sound back with one tap, or just turn it down instead of removing it completely.

## But what about safety?

Some sounds can never be blocked. Alarms, sirens, someone calling your name. They always reach you, whatever you've filtered out.

The headphones also keep an ear on the sounds you did block. Say you're working in a café and you've blocked the loud conversation at the next table. If that conversation turns into shouting, the headphones bring it back and tell you why. You chose to block the chatter, not an emergency.
