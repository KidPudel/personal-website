I created Observatory as a product-design project from the first idea through release. I shaped the features and detailed behavior, information architecture, interactions, interface, telescope artwork, graphic identity, and launch film. The application code was created with AI under my direction from the specifications and design decisions I authored.

The live view groups related processes into whole-application totals. Controlled tests record one to four applications with optional warm-up and repeated rounds, then keep their results available for later inspection and comparison. Recorded data stays on the Mac.
