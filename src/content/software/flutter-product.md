I was hired as a mobile engineer for SuperGood, PizzaSushiWok’s homemade-food delivery product. The premise was a meal that felt close to home, arriving quickly, at a price that made sense. The product was at a low: reviews attacked the application, and they attacked the food against the cost.

I proposed replacing separate Android and iOS applications with one Flutter product, learned Flutter while building it, and carried the application from an incomplete need through production readiness. The redesign covered greeting and first-run, menu behavior, basket and ordering, favourites, payment and 3DS, maps and addresses, courier tracking, promotions, loyalty, themes, and adaptive layouts.

The strongest work was not a prettier catalog. It was making the order itself carry the promise: inspectable dishes, honest time and price, and a way to eat again from history. The shipped product was easier to navigate and more usable than the apps it replaced. SuperGood later closed because of the financial situation.
